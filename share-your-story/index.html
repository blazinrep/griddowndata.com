Share your story: GridDownData Field Reports

Real preparedness is learned by doing. Field Reports is where readers share what they actually did, tested, or lived through — so the next person doesn't have to learn it the hard way.

What we're looking for

Firsthand preparedness stories and practical tips. Something you actually did, tested, or lived through — not something you read about or think might work.

  • A tip (100–300 words): one specific thing that worked for you. A water-storage trick, a cold-weather fix, a way you stretched food, fuel, or power.
  • A story (up to about 1,500 words): what happened, what you did, what worked, what didn't, and what you'd do differently next time.

Good Field Reports are specific and honest. Tell us what you tried, what the conditions were, and how it turned out — including the parts that failed. A failed attempt, told straight, is worth more than a perfect-sounding story that never happened.

Ground rules

  • Original work only. Send us your own words and your own experience.
  • No fabricated firsthand experiences. If you didn't do it, test it, or live through it, don't write it as if you did. Made-up stories are not published, even good ones.
  • No AI-generated submissions. We do not accept pieces written by AI. Using a spellchecker or grammar tool to clean up your own writing is fine — having AI write the piece for you is not.
  • No guesswork or untested ideas. If you haven't tried it, say so — or don't send it. Readers may rely on what we publish.
  • No sales pitches and no affiliate links. Field Reports are not ads. Don't pitch a product, a business, or a link that earns you money.
  • 18 or older only — or a parent or guardian submits on your behalf.

Safety scope

First-aid content in Field Reports stays at the awareness level: what happened, what you did, and what you learned. We do not publish diagnosis, dosing, or step-by-step procedure instruction from contributors.

Water, food, electrical, and fuel safety numbers are verified against published sources before anything is published. If we can't verify a number, it doesn't go in.

Educational reference material — not medical training or a substitute for professional care.

Permissions — what you're agreeing to

You keep your copyright. By submitting, you grant GridDownData a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to publish your piece on griddowndata.com, in the GridDownData email newsletter, and on GridDownData social accounts, with credit to you. Non-exclusive means you can still republish your piece anywhere else you like.

By submitting, you confirm that the work is original, that it is based on your own firsthand experience, and that any photos you later send us (only if we ask for them) are yours to share.

Privacy — what happens to your information

  • Your email is never published or shared. We use it only to follow up with you about your submission.
  • Location is optional. If you give one, a city and state or a general region is plenty. We never ask for a precise address, and you should never send one.
  • Your contact information is never exposed. Nothing you send us in the form appears on the site except the name or pen name, one-line background, and bio link you choose for publication (see Credit, below).
  • Submissions are never published automatically. Every submission is read by a person first. Nothing goes live without that review — and without us emailing you first if your piece is selected.

Editorial discretion

  • We may lightly edit a selected piece for length, clarity, and safety.
  • We may decline any submission, without explanation.
  • We may remove a published piece at any time.
  • If we correct a published piece, the correction gets a dated note on the piece itself.

Credit — how you're named

If your piece is published, it runs under your name or pen name, with a one-line background (for example, "Homesteader in East Texas" or "Retired firefighter, Mississippi") and one bio link to your own site, blog, or channel. No affiliate links.

Bio links on published Field Reports carry rel="ugc nofollow" — that is our standing link policy for contributor links, so you know before you submit.

Photographs

Photographs are not required. Send your story or tip as words — that's enough. If your submission is selected and photographs would help it, we will ask for them by email. Please don't send photos unless we ask.

What happens after you submit

  • We read every submission.
  • If yours is selected, we email you before anything publishes — to confirm details, ask about photos if they'd help, and agree on your credit line.
  • If yours isn't selected, we may not be able to reply to everyone — but every submission is read.
  • There is no payment at launch. Published contributors get credit by name (or pen name), a one-line background, and one bio link.

No account or sign-in is needed to submit — just the form below.
