A hook, a line, and a little knowledge can feed you long after the tackle shop closes.

Survival fishing is catching fish for food without conventional rod-and-reel gear, using handlines, passive fish traps, gill nets, spears, and improvised hooks. Passive methods like traps and set lines are the most calorie-efficient because they keep fishing while you handle other tasks. A handline needs only line (paracord strands, shoelaces, or cloth strips), a hook, and bait. Frequently asked questions

How do I fish without a rod and reel?

A handline is the simplest method: wrap line around a can, a stick, or your hand, tie on a hook and bait, and drop it into likely water, then feel the strike directly and pull the fish in hand over hand. Paracord inner strands, shoelaces, dental floss, or strips of cloth can all serve as line, so you can fish with almost nothing manufactured.

What is the most efficient survival fishing method?

Passive methods win on calories. A fish trap woven from sticks or a net set across a channel keeps working around the clock while you gather firewood, purify water, or sleep, catching fish without your constant attention. Because survival is about maximizing food for minimum energy spent, the guide emphasizes setting several passive devices at once rather than actively working a single line all day long.

How can I make a fishing hook without tackle?

Improvised hooks can be carved from hardwood, bone, or thorns, bent from a soda-can tab, safety pin, or scrap wire, or built as a gorge, a small toggle that turns sideways in the fish's throat once swallowed. The guide shows several designs and how to attach them securely to your line, because a hook you can fashion from your surroundings means you are never truly without fishing tackle.

Are survival fishing methods like nets and traps legal?

Usually not for everyday use. Gill nets, fish traps, spearing, and multiple set lines are restricted or prohibited under normal fishing regulations in most areas and are lawful only in a true life-or-death emergency. The guide flags this clearly and encourages learning and practicing within the law, so you understand the techniques before a crisis without risking fines or penalties in ordinary times.
