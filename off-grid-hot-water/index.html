Off-Grid Hot Water: Safe Heating Options

Off-grid water heating can use a kettle on an approved stove, solar equipment or a properly installed fuel-fired heater. Prevent scalds and carbon monoxide exposure. Never improvise a sealed or pressurized vessel over a heat source.

Plan water heating before an outage. Use equipment rated for its intended location and pressure, and get professional help for permanent plumbing, combustion or pressure-system installations.

Frequently asked questions

What is the simplest way to get hot water with no power?

Heat a manageable amount of water in a stable pot on an approved cooking appliance. Keep children away and mix with cool water before use. Use outdoor-only burners outside, never in an enclosed space.

How does a solar batch water heater work?

A solar batch heater absorbs sunlight to warm water. Use purpose-designed equipment with appropriate freeze protection, scald protection and pressure relief when required. Do not adapt an unapproved sealed tank or connect improvised equipment to household pressure.

Is a propane tankless water heater safe off grid?

Use outdoor-only propane water heaters outdoors away from openings, even if a room has ventilation. Indoor units must be specifically listed for indoor installation and properly vented. Follow manufacturer instructions and use carbon monoxide alarms.

How do I heat water without wasting fuel or risking scalds?

Heat only what you need and use stable containers. Check bathing water with a thermometer, mix to a comfortable temperature before contact and supervise children. Never block relief valves or heat a closed container.
