Ice Storm Power Outage Preparation

Freezing rain can bring down trees and power lines, leaving homes without heat or electricity. Prepare before the storm arrives: arrange safe backup heat, supplies, communication and a plan to relocate if conditions become unsafe.

Before freezing rain arrives

  • Charge phones and power banks; keep a battery radio and flashlights ready.
  • Store drinking water, easy-to-prepare food, essential medications and pet supplies.
  • Test smoke and battery-backed carbon monoxide alarms.
  • Make a household contact plan and identify a heated place to go if needed.
  • Keep appliance thermometers and a cooler available. Plan for medical devices that require electricity.

When the power goes out

Report the outage to your utility, monitor official alerts, conserve phone battery and keep refrigerator and freezer doors closed. Avoid candles when battery lighting is available. Do not use outdoor cooking or heating equipment indoors. If a carbon monoxide alarm sounds, get outside immediately and call 911.

Keep away from ice-loaded branches and downed wires. If you cannot maintain safe warmth or essential medical care, seek a heated shelter or emergency assistance. Seek emergency care for suspected hypothermia, including confusion, slurred speech or unusual drowsiness.

Frequently asked questions

What should I prepare before an ice storm?

Gather warm layers, blankets, flashlights, spare batteries, drinking water, shelf-stable food, necessary medicines and charged power banks. Arrange backup power or a safe alternate location for anyone dependent on powered medical equipment.

How can I stay warm safely without electricity?

Wear dry layers, gather in a safe room and use blankets. Use only heating appliances approved for indoor use and installed according to instructions. Never heat a home with a grill, camp stove or gas oven. Move to a heated shelter before the home becomes dangerously cold.

Where can I run a portable generator?

Run it outdoors at least 20 feet from the home, away from windows, doors and vents, with exhaust directed away from buildings. Never run it in a garage. Use suitable outdoor-rated cords or a professionally installed transfer system; never backfeed a wall outlet.

How long does refrigerated food stay safe?

With doors closed, an unpowered refrigerator generally keeps food safe for four hours; a full freezer for 48 hours and a half-full freezer for 24 hours. Use a thermometer and a cooler with ice to maintain perishables at 40°F or below. Discard unsafe perishables; never taste food to test safety.

What should I do about downed power lines?

Treat every downed line as energized. Stay well away, keep others away and report it to the utility or emergency services. Do not touch branches or objects contacting the line. Avoid unnecessary travel on icy roads.
