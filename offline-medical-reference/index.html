Frequently asked questions

What is an offline medical reference?

It is a complete emergency-medicine library stored locally on a device so it works with no internet, cell service, or power. GridDownData delivers one on an air-gapped USB drive covering bleeding control, wound care, burns, fractures, medication dosing, and infection recognition. It is designed for plain-language use by non-clinicians when professional care is delayed or unreachable.

Does it replace a doctor?

No. It is a reference to help you act when no clinician is available, and it supplements rather than replaces professional care whenever that care can be reached. The guidance helps you stabilize an injury or illness and make informed decisions, but you should still seek qualified medical treatment as soon as it becomes possible.

What emergencies does it cover?

The reference addresses the situations most likely to matter when help is far off: severe bleeding and wound management, burns, sprains and fractures with splinting, allergic reactions, dehydration, and the signs of infection or shock. Each entry explains what to assess, what to do with the supplies on hand, and the warning signs that mean a person needs advanced care urgently.

Do I need internet or power to use it?

No. The entire library is stored on the air-gapped USB drive and opens on a phone, tablet, or laptop with no network connection. Because it draws nothing from the cloud, it keeps working during grid-down events, in remote areas with no signal, and after cell infrastructure fails, which is exactly when medical references are hardest to reach.
