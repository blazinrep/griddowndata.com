Offline Medical References for Emergency Preparedness

An offline medical reference is a collection of locally saved first aid and health information that can be read without an internet connection on a compatible powered device. It supplements emergency care and training; it does not authorize diagnosis, prescription changes or invasive procedures.

Download reliable references before an outage, keep printed instructions and backup power, and seek qualified care as soon as possible. In an emergency call 911 when available.

Frequently asked questions

What is an offline medical reference?

An offline reference is a locally stored collection of first aid and medical information. Choose authoritative, current resources and keep printed backups for essential procedures.

Does it replace a doctor?

No. Seek professional care and call emergency services when available. Use first aid within your training while awaiting help; do not use reference material to change prescription doses or perform invasive procedures.

What emergencies does it cover?

Useful topics include bleeding control, burns, recognition of shock and other emergency warning signs. Learn these through hands-on training and follow dispatcher or clinician advice.

Do I need internet or power to use it?

No internet is required after downloading the files, but electronic devices need battery or other power and compatible software. Keep printed copies of essential instructions.
